# 🧮 Mathematics Foundations

A flexible sequence from mathematical language and algebra toward calculus, linear algebra and proof.

> Suggested order, not a mandatory syllabus. Adjust the depth to your prerequisites.

Build comfort with algebraic manipulation, functions and the meaning of mathematical notation.
