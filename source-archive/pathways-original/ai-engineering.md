# 🧠 AI Engineering

A high-level learning path from ML foundations to building, evaluating and maintaining AI-powered applications.

> Suggested order, not a mandatory syllabus. Adjust the depth to your prerequisites.

## 1. Establish ML foundations

Understand supervised learning, optimization and model training.

- [`ai-stanford-cs229`](../subjects/artificial-intelligence/README.md#ai-stanford-cs229) — catalog resource.
- [`ai-fastai`](../subjects/artificial-intelligence/README.md#ai-fastai) — catalog resource.

## 2. Learn modern model tooling

Study transformer concepts, model APIs and data workflows.

- [`ai-hf-learn`](../subjects/artificial-intelligence/README.md#ai-hf-learn) — catalog resource.
- [`ai-hf-transformers-course`](../subjects/artificial-intelligence/README.md#ai-hf-transformers-course) — catalog resource.

## 3. Explore language-model engineering

Connect language-model fundamentals to application patterns.

- [`ai-llm-bootcamp`](../subjects/artificial-intelligence/README.md#ai-llm-bootcamp) — catalog resource.
- [`ai-cohere-llm-university`](../subjects/artificial-intelligence/README.md#ai-cohere-llm-university) — catalog resource.

## 4. Read advanced material

Follow research seminars and framework documentation as your projects become more demanding.

- [`ai-cs25`](../subjects/artificial-intelligence/README.md#ai-cs25) — catalog resource.
